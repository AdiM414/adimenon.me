Fireguard: Autonomous Wildfire Response Robot

Lead Mechanical Designer & Autonomous Systems Integrator
📸 Place Fireguard build photo, CAD render, or GIF demo in assets/fireguard.jpg

Fireguard is an autonomous robotic ground platform engineered to navigate dangerous terrain and assess early wildfire hazards. The platform combines a custom shock-absorbing chassis, onboard thermal & optical environmental sensor fusion, and microcontrollers running autonomous path-finding algorithms to assist emergency teams without human risk.
